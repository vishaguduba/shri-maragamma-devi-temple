## Brand & Style

This design system channels the profound reverence, timeless antiquity, and spiritual sanctity of the historic Shri Maragamma Devi temple in Honnakiranagi, Kalaburagi. The interface evokes the sensation of stepping onto sun-warmed granite prakaras, the fragrance of sandalwood paste (chandana), the warmth of flickering brass deepas, and the sacred vibrancy of kumkuma and pushpa aradhana.

### Emotional Atmosphere
- **Bhakti & Tranquility:** The atmosphere is hushed, devotional, and transcendent. Layouts breathe with deliberate reverence rather than rushed information density.
- **Architectural Sanctity:** Visual rhythms draw inspiration from Kalyana Chalukya and Deccan stone pillars, brass toranas, and garbhagudi thresholds.
- **Authentic Tradition:** Avoids generic commercial gradients; instead, it relies on grounded earth tones, ceremonial pigments, and ornate stone-carving motifs.

### Design Movement
**Traditional Indian Classical & Tonal Neoclassicism:** A rich synthesis of classical temple iconography with modern high-legibility digital craftsmanship. Content rests on warm, textured sandalwood parchment backgrounds framed by low-contrast temple borders, brass filigree corner accents, and deep, resonant pigments.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from sacred temple rituals, deity alankara, and traditional temple offerings.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Kumkum Vermilion (`#8B1E0F`):** Deep, sacred vermilion representing divine feminine shakti. Used for primary CTAs, critical headers, sacred announcements, and active devotional states.
- **Secondary — Kesar / Deep Marigold (`#D96B14`):** Vibrant saffron and marigold used for ceremonial highlights, key dates, celebration banners, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary — Deep Brass & Temple Gold (`#C99738`):** Burnished temple gold representing the brass deepas, kavacha, and kireeta of the Devi. Used for ornamentation, borders, badges, and iconographic accents.
- **Neutral Surface — Chandana & Parchment (`#F9F5EC`):** A warm, tranquil sandalwood cream providing an organic, non-glaring reading surface reminiscent of sacred manuscripts.
- **Deep Text — Karikallu Charcoal (`#201915`):** Black granite charcoal mixed with warm umber to ensure long-form Kannada and English legibility without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Surface Variant — Alaya Stone (`#EFE8D8`):** Recessed panels, elevated card backdrops, and side navigation drawer fills.

## Typography

The typography system is engineered to handle bilingual scripts seamlessly: Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) script alongside English.

### Type Pairings & Script Harmony
- **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers classical weight, dignity, and ritual gravitas. Perfect for temple names, stotras, utsava titles, and sanctum announcements. Matches the sculpted curvature of classical Kannada inscriptions.
- **Body & Labels (`Noto Sans`):** Selected for pristine legibility across small print, puja rate cards, seva booking forms, and historical narratives. It effortlessly balances complex Kannada conjunct consonants (ottakshara) without clipping.

### Typesetting Rules
- Kannada script requires generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.8`) to comfortably display subscript consonants and vowel signs without collision.
- Headlines in Kannada should avoid aggressive letter-spacing; preserve natural ligature flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout mirrors temple mandapa architecture: balanced, centered, progressive, and welcoming.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop:** 12-column grid within a maximum content container of `1280px`. Central sanctum-style framing gives central content prominence.
- **Tablet:** 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margin.
- **Mobile:** 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` outer margin for thumb-reach accessibility.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Prakara Margins:** Outer margins are generous to frame content like an inner temple courtyard.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Generous section spacing (`space-xl` and above) separates different sevas, daily darshan timings, and festival schedules to evoke unhurried contemplation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial glassmorphism in favor of natural physical depth, inspired by stone courtyards, raised wooden pithas, and polished brass medallions.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Prakara Base (Level 0):** Unraised background in Sandalwood Parchment (`#F9F5EC`).
2. **Peetha / Raised Plinths (Level 1):** Subtle 1px gold-tinted hairline border (`rgba(201, 151, 56, 0.35)`) accompanied by a diffuse warm shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(80, 40, 15, 0.05)`.
3. **Mandapa Cards (Level 2):** Elevated interactive cards (Seva cards, Donation tiers) use a warm-cast shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(80, 40, 15, 0.09)` with a 1px solid antique gold border (`#E4D3A2`).
4. **Modals & Prasada Drawers (Level 3):** Deep ritual focus with a dark umber scrim (`rgba(32, 25, 21, 0.65)`) and floating containers elevated by `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(32, 25, 21, 0.25)`.

### Decorative Borders
- Top and bottom edges of major hero banners and modal dialogues utilize traditional twin-line borders or delicate temple torana motifs etched in Tertiary Gold.

## Shapes

The shape system employs Soft (`1`) roundedness (base radius `0.25rem` / `4px`). 

### Aesthetic Rationale
- Soft, disciplined geometry echoes handcrafted temple architecture, carved stone lintels, and copper inscriptions.
- Pure circles are reserved strictly for sacred medallions, deity thumbnails, deepa badges, and circular yantra motifs.
- Buttons, input containers, and structural content cards use subtle, firm corner radii rather than playful pill shapes, maintaining structural dignity.

## Components

### 1. Devotional Buttons
- **Primary (Seva / Darshan Booking):** Solid Kumkum Vermilion background (`#8B1E0F`), pure sandalwood text (`#F9F5EC`), 1px subtle gold internal border highlight. Soft radius (`4px`). On hover, deepens to rich maroon with a gentle brass outer halo.
- **Secondary (Read Sthala Purana / Details):** Sandalwood fill with a 1.5px antique gold border (`#C99738`) and vermilion typography.
- **Text Action:** Bold gold or vermilion accompanied by a traditional arrow or floral chevron (`॥`).

### 2. Seva & Darshan Cards
- Surface background in tinted Alaya Stone (`#EFE8D8`) with top arch or dual gold hairline borders.
- Header with Kannada seva title (`ಅಭಿಷೇಕ ಸೇವೆ`) in bold serif, followed by timings and dakshina/kanike amount highlighted in deep vermilion.
- Dedicated icon slot representing kalasha, deepa, or bilva patra.

### 3. Temple Notice & Panchanga Chips
- Compact pills with Sandalwood background, bordered with `#D96B14` (Marigold).
- Used for indicating auspicious tithi, nakshatra, temple timings (e.g., "ಮುಂಜಾನೆ ೬:೦೦ - ಮಧ್ಯಾಹ್ನ ೧:೩೦"), and festival tags.

### 4. Input Fields & Kanike (Donation) Forms
- Crisp, respectful inputs with `#FDFBF7` background and 1px muted brass border (`#D8C6A5`).
- Active focus state: 1.5px Kumkum Vermilion outline with no harsh glow, respecting spiritual calm.
- Prefix slots designed for Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) and devotional gotra/nakshatra selectors.

### 5. Daily Timetable & Seva Lists
- Clean alternating rows using subtle Sandalwood Parchment and Pale Sandstone tints.
- Divided by hairline borders styled like traditional brass filigree dividers (`rgba(201, 151, 56, 0.25)`).

### 6. Devotional Motifs & Dividers
- Section dividers feature a central brass deepa or mangala kalasha silhouette flanked by tapered horizontal lines.
